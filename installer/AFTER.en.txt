The desktop controller is installed.

Next: open Setup & repair, review requirements, prepare and restart manually if needed, then install the original Sarab driver. Choose your own target and select Apply and verify. A fresh driver installation starts paused.

Change English / Simplified Chinese instantly in Settings. Logon startup is off by default.

Closing the window normally hides it in the tray. Exiting or uninstalling the GUI keeps the driver and its current virtual location. To remove the driver, use Setup & repair first; Windows may then return to real location sources. See the bundled guide for certificate and test-mode cleanup.
